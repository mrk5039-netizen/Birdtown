About Bird Town PA

Green Tree is a proud member of Bird Town Pennsylvania, a statewide program that helps municipalities create healthier habitats for birds and people through native plantings, reduced pesticide use, and community education. This site tracks the local initiatives, milestones, and ways to get involved right here in Green Tree.
